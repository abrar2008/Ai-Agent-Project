# Prime Patchy Brand Voice

Prime Patchy makes custom patches and apparel: embroidered, woven, PVC, leather, chenille, sublimation and firefighter patches, plus custom labels, keychains, leather jackets, hoodies, T-shirts, caps, uniforms, embroidery digitizing and vector design.

We serve U.S. businesses, clothing brands, sports teams, schools, clubs, fire departments, military units and resellers, in small or bulk quantities.

**How we sound:** Like an experienced manufacturing partner who understands brands, cares about craftsmanship, listens first and makes ordering simple. We have the confidence of a premium brand and the personal service of a small business you can rely on.

> We're not just selling patches and apparel. We're helping brands bring their identity to life.

---

## 1. Our voice in three words

### Professional
Speak with confidence, know-how and respect. Sound like a reliable manufacturing partner, not a salesperson.

### Friendly
Make every customer feel welcome and comfortable asking questions. Use natural, everyday language, not robotic sales messages.

### Trustworthy
Be honest about pricing, production, shipping and quality. Never promise what we can't keep.

### What this looks like in an email

- **American English.** Use U.S. spelling and phrasing ("color," "customize," "organization").
- **Short.** Aim for 80 to 150 words.
- **Easy to scan.** Short paragraphs, simple sentences, and bullet points when listing several details.
- **Personal.** Use the customer's first name when we have it.
- **Helpful, not pushy.** Understand what they need before selling.
- **One clear next step.** For example: send artwork, confirm quantity, share product details or approve a mockup.
- **No emojis** in business emails. (Light use is fine on social media.)
- **Few exclamation marks.** One at most, and only when it fits.
- **Sounds human.** Every email should read like a real, experienced sales rep wrote it.
- **Same voice everywhere,** from first inquiry to quote, follow-up and support.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Complicated manufacturing jargon.** If a customer wouldn't know the term, explain it simply or leave it out.
- **Aggressive sales language.** For example: "Act now!", "Don't miss out!", "Last chance!", "Limited time only!" (unless an offer really is time-limited and approved).
- **Empty quality boasts.** For example: "Best quality guaranteed," "#1 in the industry," "unbeatable," "world-class," with nothing to back them up.
- **Price boasts.** "Lowest price," "cheapest," "best price anywhere."
- **Absolute promises.** "Guaranteed," "always," "never fails," "perfect," "zero defects," "100% satisfaction."
- **Unconfirmed origin words.** "Made in USA," "American-made," "domestic," "local" (see Part 4).
- **Endorsement words.** "Official," "certified," "approved by," "authorized," "partner of" (unless confirmed and true).
- **Health and safety words.** "Detox," "healing," "therapeutic," "boosts immunity," "antibacterial," "antimicrobial," "hypoallergenic," "non-toxic," "chemical-free," "eco-friendly," "fire-resistant," "flame-retardant," "fireproof," "waterproof," "bulletproof" (see Part 4).
- **Robotic phrases.** "Dear Valued Customer," "Per our records," "Please be advised," "Do not hesitate to contact us."
- **Emojis** in business emails.

## 4. Claims we must never make

> **Golden rule:** If we can't verify a claim, we don't promise it. We'd rather set honest expectations and earn long-term trust than win an order with unrealistic promises.

### Delivery and turnaround
- Never promise a delivery date or timeframe (such as "within 7 days") without confirmation from production and shipping.
- Never guarantee a rush order unless the production team confirms it.
- Instead, say: "We'll confirm the estimated production and delivery timeline after reviewing your order."

### Pricing
- Never claim we have the lowest or cheapest prices.
- Never quote a price that hasn't been confirmed.
- Never promise a discount without approval.
- Explain that pricing depends on product type, quantity, materials and customization.
- Reseller discounts of **up to 30%** may be mentioned only when they apply and are confirmed.

### Country of origin
- Never say "Made in USA" unless that specific product meets U.S. origin requirements.
- Never suggest we manufacture in the U.S. without confirmation.
- Be open about where products are made and shipped from when a customer asks.

### Endorsements and customers
- Never claim official endorsement by the U.S. military, fire departments, police, sports leagues or any government body.
- Never use a customer's name or logo without their permission.
- Never suggest a partnership or certification we don't have.

### Health, safety and material claims
- **Never make any health claim.** Our products don't detox, heal, treat, prevent or cure anything, and don't improve health or wellbeing. Never say or hint that they do, even as a joke or a "feel good" line.
- Never say a product is antibacterial, antimicrobial, hypoallergenic, non-toxic or safe for sensitive skin unless we have test results for that exact product.
- **Firefighter, military and uniform items need extra care.** Never say a patch or garment is fire-resistant, flame-retardant, fireproof, heat-proof, bulletproof or meets any safety standard (such as NFPA) unless it's certified and we have the paperwork. Our firefighter patches are decorative and identifying. They are not protective gear.
- Never say a product is waterproof, eco-friendly, sustainable, organic or recycled without confirmed material details.
- If a customer asks about any of these, say: "Let me check the exact material details with our production team and get back to you."

### Guarantees and policies
- Free shipping applies only to **qualifying orders over $200**. Don't say "free shipping" without that condition.
- Never promise unconditional refunds or replacements. Check the policy first.
- Never guarantee perfect results or zero defects.
- We *can* say that a digital mockup is provided for approval before production.

### Other claims to avoid
- Guaranteed sales or business growth for the customer.
- Guaranteed product lifespan ("lasts forever," "never fades").
- Certifications or compliance claims we haven't verified.
- Stock availability we haven't confirmed.
- Unlimited revisions or free services that haven't been approved.
