# The Steep Club Launch: 4-Email Sequence

The email copy is in `emails/`. This file covers timing, who gets each email, subject line options and what to track.

**Goal:** 300 waitlist members start a paid subscription by Sunday, January 3, 2027.
**Who gets it:** Everyone on the waitlist (about 2,000 people) who hasn't joined yet.
**Story across the 4 emails:** It's open → here's what's in the box → your questions answered → a last, low-pressure recap.

## Founding member offer (only if the owner approves it)

The emails are written **without** an offer, so they make no deadline claims. If the owner approves an offer and an end date, make these swaps:

**Email 1:** add this line after the bullet list:
> Founding member offer: join by Sunday, January 3 and get [OFFER]. It's only for people on our waitlist.

**Email 4:** replace the subject, preview and first line:
- Subject: `Our founding member offer ends tomorrow`
- Preview: `Join by Sunday, January 3. Just for our waitlist.`
- First line: "A quick reminder: the founding member offer for The Steep Club ends tomorrow, Sunday, January 3, at [END TIME] [TIME ZONE]. Join before then and you'll get [OFFER]."
- Change the price bullet to "[PRICE] a month after [OFFER PERIOD]".

Don't make these swaps unless the offer and its end date are approved. The voice guide only allows deadline language for a real, approved offer.

## Rules for sending

- **Exit:** Anyone who starts a paid subscription leaves the sequence right away. The welcome email takes over.
- **Unfinished checkout:** Anyone who starts checkout but doesn't finish gets the automatic reminder 24 hours later. It's separate from this sequence.
- **Don't send to:** People who unsubscribed, people who already joined, or anyone on a spam complaint list.
- **No re-entry:** Nobody gets the sequence twice. People who don't join get a decision on Jan 4 (for example, a gentle follow-up in February). Email 4 says it's the last email "about the launch," so a later follow-up is still honest.
- **No sends** on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day or New Year's Day. No more than 2 emails a week.
- **Drafts only.** The owner loads and sends these in their own email tool. Claude never sends, schedules or test-sends.

## A/B tests

1. **Email 1 subject line:** A ("The Steep Club is open") vs. B ("{{first_name}}, The Steep Club is open"). Split 50/50, or test on 20% and send the winner to the rest. The winner is the one with more clicks, not just more opens.
2. **Email 4 subject line:** A ("A last note about The Steep Club") vs. B ("Still deciding on The Steep Club?"). Same method. Measure by sign-ups within 48 hours.
3. **Email 2 length** (if there's time): the full email vs. a shorter version with only the box photo, the brewing card line and the button. Measure by sign-ups, not clicks.

## What to track

Targets come from the plan (section 7). They're our own goals, not industry benchmarks.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Open rate per email | 40% or higher |
| Click rate per email | 5% or higher |
| Unsubscribes per email | Under 0.5% |
| Spam complaints per email | Under 0.1% |
| Sign-ups | 120 by Dec 13, 195 by Dec 20, 300 by Jan 3 |

Check every Monday. If we're under 100 sign-ups on Dec 14, use the spare slot. Low clicks mean the email is the problem. Good clicks but low sign-ups mean the page or price is the problem.
