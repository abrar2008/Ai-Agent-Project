# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` holds the business's voice, style guide, logos and product facts.
- `leads/` holds notes about audiences, customer segments and contact lists.
- `campaigns/` holds campaign plans, email sequences and finished email drafts, with one folder per campaign.
- `notes/` holds research, decisions and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words, and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Drafting and planning are fine, but don't send, schedule or test-send to any real address.
- Ask me before deleting anything, including files, folders and drafts.
